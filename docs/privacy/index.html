Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version: TITAN and VALKYRIE collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

This policy covers the iPhone apps TITAN and VALKYRIE (together, "the apps"), published by 2747902 Alberta Ltd.

Information we collect

None. The apps do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information, and there is no account to create.

Information you enter

The apps let you record training information — workouts, sets, weights, repetitions, routines, personal records, supplements, body weight and body measurements. This information:

  • is stored only in the app's own storage on your device;
  • is never transmitted anywhere, because the apps make no network requests;
  • is never seen by the developer, who has no way to access it;
  • is not shared with, or sold to, anyone.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, that backup is made by Apple and may include the apps' data, in the same way it includes data from your other apps. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The apps contain no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. Nothing you do in the apps is measured, profiled or reported. No data is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Health data

The apps do not connect to Apple Health and do not read from or write to it. Training and body measurements you enter are treated the same as any other information you type into the apps: stored on your device only.

Children

The apps are rated 4+ and are safe for all ages. Because no information is collected from anyone, no information is collected from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing for you to request deletion of and no account to close.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the apps changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised before that version is released.
