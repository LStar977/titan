**Description** (4000 max — ~1900 used)

```
TITAN is a strength training log built for one thing: getting your sets recorded fast, so you can get back to lifting.

LOG WITHOUT THINKING
Every set opens pre-filled with what you did last time — tap the checkmark and move on. Type an exact weight when you need to, or use the steppers. Change a weight once and it carries forward to the rest of your sets. The rest timer starts on its own the moment you finish a set.

KNOW WHEN YOU'VE HIT SOMETHING
TITAN tracks your estimated one-rep max on every lift and flags a personal record the moment you beat it. No spreadsheets, no math, no digging back through old sessions to check.

BUILD YOUR SPLIT
Create your own routines, or start from a proven program — Push/Pull/Legs, Starting Strength, StrongLifts 5x5, or Upper/Lower. Set your split and TITAN tells you which day comes next, at your pace, not on a fixed calendar.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE
A muscle heat map shows what you have actually trained — front and back, male or female model. Estimated 1RM charts, weekly volume, volume by muscle group, and a full history calendar you can tap into for any session, set by set.

CLIMB THE RANKS
Twelve levels from Bronze to Titan. Every workout, every working set and every PR earns XP. A small thing that makes showing up on a bad day slightly easier.

EVERYTHING ELSE
- 80+ exercises including cardio and stretching, plus your own custom lifts
- Supersets you can set on individual sets, not just whole exercises
- Plate calculator for what to load on each side of the bar
- Supplement tracking with a calendar
- Body weight and measurements with trend charts
- Bodyweight exercises count toward your training volume

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. No sign-up. No ads. No tracking. TITAN collects nothing, and your training history stays on your phone.
```

## App Review Information

**Sign-in required:** No

**Notes**

```
TITAN is a fully offline strength-training log. There is no account system and no sign-in, so no demo credentials are needed — the app is fully functional the moment it launches.

All data is stored locally on device using SwiftData. The app makes no network requests and collects no user data.

To try the main flow: tap the centre dumbbell tab to start a workout, add an exercise, enter a weight and reps, then tap the checkmark to log the set. The rest timer starts automatically and personal records are flagged as they happen.
```

## Screenshots

Required: 6.9" iPhone display — 1290 × 2796 or 1320 × 2868 portrait. Up to 10,
minimum 1. Every other size is generated from these.
