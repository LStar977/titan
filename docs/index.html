Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start logging.

Where is my training data stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. The app has no servers and makes no network requests.

Will I lose my history when the app updates?

No. Updates keep everything — workouts, routines, records and body measurements all carry over.

How do I back up my data?

Your workout history is included in your normal iPhone backup, so if you back up to iCloud or to a computer it is covered. Deleting the app deletes its data from your device.

Does it work without internet?

Entirely. The app never needs a connection, which is useful in a gym basement with no signal.

Can I add exercises that are not in the list?

Yes. Search for the exercise you want when adding one, and if nothing matches you will be offered the option to create it.

I found a bug, or I want a feature added.

Email the address above. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when it happened, and it will get looked at.
